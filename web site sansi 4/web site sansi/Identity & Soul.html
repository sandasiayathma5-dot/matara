Matara has a rich cultural identity influenced by religion, history and local traditions. Buddhist religious activities have an important place in the community, while different religious and cultural groups contribute to the diversity of the city


Religious & Cultural Events

Religious festivals and cultural events bring communities together. Temples, devalayas and other religious places are important parts of the cultural landscape.
Traditional ceremonies, processions and community activities also contribute to the identity of Matara.


Local Lifestyle

The lifestyle of people in Matara is connected with both urban and coastal activities.

Important activities include:

  • Fishing
  • Trading
  • Tourism
  • Small businesses
  • Agriculture
  • Government and private sector employment
  • Education

Local markets are also important places where people buy and sell food, fish, vegetables and other goods.


Local Food

Matara has a food culture influenced by southern Sri Lankan traditions. Rice and curry, seafood, coconut-based dishes and traditional sweets are common parts of the local food culture.


Natural Environment

Matara has a valuable natural environment consisting of:
  • Coastal areas
  • Beaches
  • Nilwala River
  • Wetlands
  • Coconut plantations
  • Marine ecosystems
  • Trees and vegetation

Environmental Challenges

Rapid urban development and human activities can create environmental problems. Coastal pollution, waste disposal, water pollution and loss of natural areas can affect the environment.
Protecting the coastal environment, rivers, wetlands and green spaces is important for the sustainable development of Matara.

Sustainable Matara

Future development should balance economic growth, environmental protection and the needs of local communities.
A sustainable Matara should have:
  • Clean and safe public spaces
  • Protected natural areas
  • Better waste management
  • Environmentally friendly transport
  • Protection of historical buildings
  • Green spaces
  • Community participation
