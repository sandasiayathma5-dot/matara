Education System

Education plays an important role in the social and economic development of Matara. The city and surrounding areas have many government schools, national schools, private educational institutions and vocational training opportunities.
Students can receive education from primary level to secondary level and continue towards higher education and professional training.

Higher & Vocational Education

Matara also provides opportunities for students who want to continue their education after school.
